Present Development – RTFF & RTDAS

Increasing frequency of flood events in the Chennai region demands a robust real-time response system. The project implements:

  • RTFF (Real-Time Flood Forecasting) — hydrologic-hydraulic modelling for real-time flood warnings
  • RTDAS (Real-Time Data Acquisition System) — collects and transmits sensor data (rainfall, water levels) in real time

Together they enhance early warning capabilities and support decision-making for flood management authorities.

📡
RTDAS

Real-Time Data Acquisition System — sensor network collecting rainfall and water-level data continuously.

🌊
RTFF

Real-Time Flood Forecasting — hydrologic-hydraulic model driven by live sensor data for flood prediction.

⚠️
Early Warning

Integrated SDSS (Spatial Decision Support System) for actionable flood alerts to authorities and communities.

Resilient Hydro Twin (RHT)

🏙 Scope
  • Development of a city-scale digital twin integrating hydrological, hydraulic, urban infrastructure, water resource, governance, and human behaviour models
  • Incorporates participatory governance, enabling planners and communities to co-create flood response strategies
📈 Expected Outcomes
  • Accurate flood predictions, visualization of runoff, identification of vulnerable zones, data-driven adaptation responses
  • Promotes data-driven, stakeholder-collaborative flood management and planning

Reservoir Flood Mitigation

An improved modelling framework for the simulation, optimisation and operation of small and medium reservoirs for flood mitigation.

Chennai Floods — 2015
~100 Mm³

Chembarambakkam reservoir overflow triggered catastrophic urban flooding across Chennai, highlighting the need for real-time reservoir operation.

Pune Floods — 2024
~60 Mm³

Khadakwasla Dam releases caused severe downstream flooding in Pune, demonstrating the critical role of optimised dam operation.

ℹ Need for flood control of small and medium reservoirs used for conservation purpose located just upstream of Indian cities.
